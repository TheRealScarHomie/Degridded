Degridded: Free yourself from gridded object tyranny and embrace the freedom of being able to place curved walls and make any building you want. Made because I wanted a 2x2 curved wall and didnt think placing curved planks one by one was a smart solution to it, not to mention but the vanilla walls look really good even when scaled and make it yourself curved walls do not, also more creative freedom == BETTER! 

You can find the ungridded versions of objects in the scenery tab as normal objects, just search for their name or the one you want ungridded and it'll appear.

Warning: Objects wont load if this mod is installed and then uninstalled or potentially worse, use at your own risk and always back up pre-modded saves, let me know of any bugs in the issues section of the github page. Also its unlikely that sharing blueprints works if these are included, or they could just load in blank, singleplayer use only.

Thanks to Coppertines TMS Indev code for inspiration with this, that mod crashed my game every time I opened it but I knew the feature was in there probably so with way too much trial and error I finally cracked it, feel free to use this mod to fix that bug, either way this is not an independent thing anywhere and I thought it would be better as its own thing since its kinda scope creep and I hope you enjoy!
